✦ YOUR GUIDE TO THE UNIVERSE

How to Play

One shared screen, everyone together. Choose your conversation, discover an activity, and enjoy the moment.

1♧

Set Up Your Game

On Home, choose Friends or Family, then select 10, 20, or Unlimited turns. Press Start Playing.

2✦

Discover an Activity

Press Start Turn. The Universe randomly reveals one of four activities: Question Zone, Pick Your Fate, Random Event, or Hot Seat. If Hot Seat appears, the group chooses the participant together.

3◎

Continue the Fun

When the group is ready, choose Complete or Skip. Only completed activities count toward a 10- or 20-activity target. Unlimited continues until all available content is exhausted or the group ends the game.

✧ Meet the Four Activities

☷ Question Zone

A conversation question appears for everyone. Discuss it together. Use Next Question if you want another unused question without ending the turn.

◉ Pick Your Fate

Choose Truth to answer a question, Challenge to try a playful task, or Mystery to let the game choose Truth or Challenge.

◎ Hot Seat

Hot Seat is one of the four activities that can be selected randomly. When it appears, the group chooses one person to be in the spotlight; everyone else takes turns asking questions. The app never assigns a person, and the participant may pass.

✧ Random Event

A surprise group prompt appears. It may ask everyone to answer, invite one person, reveal a surprise question, or give the group a double challenge.

Let's Talk is designed for face-to-face play around one shared device. Be kind, respect everyone's comfort, and feel free to pass on a prompt.
