YOUR GUIDE TO THE UNIVERSE

How to Play

Four activities. One shared device. Endless conversations.

01

Start a Turn

Press Start Turn to summon a random activity from the Universe.

02

Play Together

Follow the instructions and enjoy the activity face-to-face.

03

Complete or Skip

Complete the activity or skip it to move to the next turn.

DISCOVER THE FOUR ACTIVITIES

☷

Question Zone

QUESTIONS

Discover a question and share your thoughts, memories, and experiences with the group.

  • One question appears at a time.
  • Use Next Question to reveal another unused question.
  • Complete when the conversation is finished.
◉

Pick Your Fate

CHOICES

Choose a portal and discover your next experience.

  • Truth: Answer an honest question.
  • Challenge: Complete a fun challenge.
  • Mystery: Let the system choose Truth or Challenge.
◎

Hot Seat

SPOTLIGHT

One person takes the spotlight while everyone else asks questions.

  • Hot Seat can appear as one of the four randomly selected activities.
  • The group verbally chooses who takes the spotlight; the app never assigns a person.
  • Everyone else takes turns asking questions, and the selected person may pass.
✧

Random Event

SURPRISE

A surprise activity brings a new twist to the conversation.

  • Everyone Answers: Everyone answers one question.
  • Pick a Player: Choose one person to participate.
  • Surprise Question: Reveal an unexpected question.
  • Double Challenge: Complete two challenges.
✦

No pressure. No scores. No winners. Respect everyone's comfort and enjoy the conversation.
